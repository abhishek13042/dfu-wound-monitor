    How far to trust a result

    Dice is the overlap between the model's region and a clinician's outline, from 0 to 1. The gap between the two numbers is the honest summary: on photos unlike the training set, the region can be badly wrong. Treat every result here as a research measurement.

    Runs entirely in this browser with ONNX Runtime Web. No account, no stored cases and no server analysis. Model spec-02-03-resnet34-unet-512-onnx-v1.
